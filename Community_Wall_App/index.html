💡 Small things that help

💡 Tip

You are not a burden

The people who love you would rather carry some of your weight than watch you carry all of it alone. You are allowed to lean.

💡 Tip

The 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique

When anxiety feels overwhelming: name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste.

💙 Did you know?

1 in 5 adults experiences a mental illness each year

Mental health conditions are among the most common health conditions in the world. You are not alone. You are not unusual. You are human.

💙 Did you know?

Sleep and mental health are deeply connected

Even small improvements — a consistent bedtime, no screens 30 minutes before bed — can meaningfully shift how you feel.
